Sources, method & responsible use

What runs today

PathFinder is a functional, deterministic recommendation baseline. It has no runtime LLM, trained forecasting model, automatic monitoring, recruitment integration or employment guarantee. Five role pathways and nine foundational skills form a small curated catalogue. AI model integration is a future extension, not a deployed feature.

Scoring and uncertainty

Moderate: 65 × skill coverage + 20 × interest alignment + 15 × signal relevance. Fast: 55 × weighted skill coverage + 20 × interest alignment + 25 × signal relevance. Fast gives Machine learning, Automation and AI ethics a 1.5 skill priority. All weights are explicit design assumptions.

Signal relevance averages keyword shares for the role’s required skills. Jobs weight 70% and AI discussion 30%; if a source is unavailable, the remaining source receives the full signal weight. Synthetic imports use only synthetic records for scoring. No signals means zero signal points, without reassigning them to other components. Scores are rounded to 0–100 and are not probabilities.

Directions compare two publication-date groups within the same source, with at least five records each. No cross-source direction comparisons. A keyword’s absence is not proof of skill obsolescence.

Data and attribution

Arbeitnow public job API: public title/tag metadata, Europe-heavy. Public access does not imply an unrestricted open-data licence; no full descriptions are retained or redistributed. Hacker News Search by Algolia: public AI-discussion title/link/date metadata, not an authoritative research feed. Publisher rights remain with publishers.

Role references link to O*NET® OnLine, USDOL/ETA, CC BY 4.0. Role-skill subsets, scores and learning estimates are project-specific simplifications and have not been endorsed or tested by USDOL/ETA. Course links retain provider terms; introductory content and catalogues are distinguished from certifications. UN SDGs ground the mappings; they do not endorse this product.

Privacy and fairness

Use synthetic personas only. The app has no CV upload, email field, profile backend or AI API. Browser storage keeps selected persona, skills, progress and signal metadata until reset or browser deletion. Feed calls send no profile values, but providers see ordinary network metadata. Hosting may process normal service logs. No age, gender, ethnicity or university prestige enters scoring. Fairness across groups has not been empirically evaluated.
